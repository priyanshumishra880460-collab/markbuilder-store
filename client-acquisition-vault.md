## MODULE 1: THE CORE OUTREACH ARCHITECTURE
Every high-converting cold email follows a 4-part psychological structure:

1. **The Specific Context Hook (0–3 seconds):** Prove immediately that you are not a bot and that you looked at their actual business. Never open with "My name is...".
2. **The Identified Bottleneck (Agitation):** Mention one clear area of improvement in their current setup (e.g., slow load speed, outdated landing page copy, missing mobile CTA).
3. **The Low-Friction Solution (Value Drop):** Provide a 1-sentence solution or attach a 60-second Loom video / preview mockup.
4. **The Frictionless CTA (Call to Action):** Never ask for a 30-minute call upfront. Ask for permission to share a free resource or an open-ended question (e.g., *"Open to taking a look at a 2-minute preview?"*).
