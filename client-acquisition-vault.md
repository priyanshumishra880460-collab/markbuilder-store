## MODULE 1: THE CORE OUTREACH ARCHITECTURE
Every high-converting cold email follows a 4-part psychological structure:

1. **The Specific Context Hook (0–3 seconds):** Prove immediately that you are not an automated spam bot. Never open with *"My name is..."* or *"I hope this email finds you well."* Start directly with an observation about their product.
2. **The Identified Bottleneck (Agitation):** Mention one clear, tangible friction point in their current business (e.g., slow mobile load speed, unoptimized checkout drop-off, missing retention pacing in videos).
3. **The Low-Friction Value Drop:** Provide a 1-sentence solution or attach a 60-second Loom screen recording / Figma teaser.
4. **The Frictionless CTA (Call to Action):** Never ask for a 30-minute Zoom call upfront. Ask for permission to share a 2-minute free breakdown (e.g., *"Open to taking a look at a 90-second teardown? No strings attached."*).

### The 5 Deadly Mistakes That Trigger Instant Deletion:
- **Mistake #1: Writing a Life Story:** Founders don't care where you studied or your resume. They care about their problem.
- **Mistake #2: Vague Compliments:** *"I love your website!"* feels fake. *"Noticed your mobile hero image layout shifts on iPhone 14"* feels expert.
- **Mistake #3: Heavy Attachments:** Large zip files or unsolicited PDFs trigger spam filters. Use lightweight cloud links.
- **Mistake #4: Asking for Marriage on the First Date:** Demanding a 45-minute calendar booking immediately creates massive friction.
- **Mistake #5: Zero Follow-Up:** 80% of closed contracts come from follow-ups #2 through #5. Giving up after 1 email means leaving 80% of revenue on the table.
