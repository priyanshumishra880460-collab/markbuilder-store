DATE: [Date]
PREPARED BY: [Your Name / Agency Name]
PROJECT: [E.g., Custom Responsive Web Platform & Payment Gateway]

1. TARGET OBJECTIVE
Deliver a production-ready, high-converting digital platform within 21 business days to increase lead conversions by an estimated 25-30%.

2. 3-PHASE EXECUTION TIMELINE
• Phase 1: Wireframing & UX Approval (Days 1–5)
• Phase 2: Core Development & Integrations (Days 6–16)
• Phase 3: QA, Performance Audit & Deployment (Days 17–21)

3. DELIVERABLES & SCOPE
• Included: Fully responsive mobile/desktop application, Stripe/Razorpay integration, CMS setup, and 14 days of post-launch bug support.
• Revisions: 2 rounds of consolidated feedback per phase.
• Excluded: Unplanned third-party API subscription costs, features requested outside written scope.

4. INVESTMENT & PAYMENT TERMS
• Total Project Fee: [Amount: ₹XX,XXX / $X,XXX]
• Milestone 1: 50% non-refundable deposit prior to project kick-off.
• Milestone 2: 50% upon final acceptance prior to code/DNS handover.
• Validity: This proposal is valid for 7 business days.

⚖️ Module 5: Master Freelance & Agency Service Agreement

Standard legal contract template. Protects your IP, prevents scope creep, and guarantees payment.

MASTER PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on [Date] by and between:
SERVICE PROVIDER: [Your Name / Agency Name] ("Provider")
CLIENT: [Client Company Name] ("Client")

1. SERVICES: Provider agrees to deliver the services described in the agreed Statement of Work. Additional features outside scope will require a signed Change Order at additional fees.

2. PAYMENT & DEPOSIT:
• 50% non-refundable deposit is required prior to project kick-off.
• Remaining 50% is due immediately upon final completion before release of production files or master credentials.
• Invoices unpaid after 7 calendar days incur 1.5% monthly late interest.

3. REVISIONS: Client receives up to two (2) rounds of consolidated written revisions. Failure to provide feedback within 5 business days constitutes deemed acceptance.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY: Upon receipt of FULL AND FINAL PAYMENT, all IP rights transfer to Client. Until full payment is received, Provider retains 100% ownership of all source code, designs, and assets. Client is prohibited from publishing unpaid deliverables.

5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR: Provider is an independent contractor. No employer-employee relationship is created.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY: Provider's total aggregate liability under this agreement shall not exceed total fees paid by Client.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties execute this Agreement:
Provider Signature: __________________   Date: ________
Client Signature:   __________________   Date: ________
